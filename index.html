13 payslips, not 12

Italian salaries are paid over 13 installments. But local taxes stop after month 11, and the 13th payslip carries no tax credits at all — so your take-home actually changes shape across the year.

Assumptions used in this model

This calculator simplifies Italy's payroll rules to a single standard case. The figures above only hold under the following conditions.

  1. 01Permanent, full-time employee — corporate / tech sector
  2. 02Entered the workforce after 1996 (INPS contributive cap applies)
  3. 03Resident and working in Milan, Lombardia
  4. 04Paid over 13 monthly installments (no 14th — standard for tech)
  5. 05No CCNL — so no supplementary health fund deduction
  6. 06Full 365-day tax year, no special exemptions or bonuses
  7. 07Not first year of Italian residency (regional tax applies)
  8. 08December figures exclude year-end conguaglio adjustment

Built as a working prototype of Italy's payroll logic — INPS, IRPEF, regional & municipal surtax, cuneo fiscale. Simplified for a single standard case: permanent, full-time, Milan-based, post-1996 entrant, no CCNL.
